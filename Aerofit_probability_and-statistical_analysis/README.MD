# Aerofit Treadmill Customer Profiling & Probability Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project leverages Descriptive Statistics and Probability to profile customers of Aerofit treadmills. By analyzing demographic and behavioral data, the goal is to determine the marginal and conditional probabilities of a customer purchasing a specific treadmill model (KP281, KP481, or KP781).

## 📉 Business Problem
Aerofit needs to identify the characteristics of their target audience for each treadmill model to tailor their marketing campaigns effectively. The objective is to understand which customer demographics (age, income, gender, fitness level) are most likely to purchase beginner, intermediate, or advanced treadmill models.

## 🛠 Methodology
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Univariate and bivariate analysis using Python (Pandas, Seaborn) to uncover data distributions and detect outliers.
* **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculated measures of central tendency (mean, median) and dispersion (standard deviation, IQR) for customer attributes.
* **Probability Calculations:** Computed marginal probabilities, joint probabilities, and conditional probabilities to establish relationships between customer profiles and product choices.
