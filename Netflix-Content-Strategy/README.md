# Netflix Content Analysis & Strategic Recommendations

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes Netflix’s catalogue to identify content production strategies and growth opportunities across different countries[cite: 1]. By examining distribution trends, the goal is to provide actionable business recommendations for content expansion[cite: 1].

## 📉 Business Problem
To maintain market dominance, Netflix needs to understand historical release trends and optimize its content acquisition strategy. The primary objectives are to evaluate the changing volume of movie releases, compare TV shows versus movies, identify the optimal launch timing for series, and understand regional content availability[cite: 1].

## 🛠 Methodology
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Conducted using Python (Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, Matplotlib)[cite: 1].
* **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing data, extracted date features (years/months), and performed outlier detection on movie durations using the Interquartile Range (IQR)[cite: 1].
* **Feature Engineering:** Split multi-valued genres and country lists for granular categorical analysis[cite: 1].

## 📊 Key Findings
* **Content Growth:** The Netflix library grew exponentially after 2015[cite: 1]. 
* **Format Dominance:** Movies dominate the platform, making up approximately 70% of the total catalogue[cite: 1].
* **Top Categories:** The United States, India, and the United Kingdom are the leading content producers, with Drama, International, and Comedy being the most popular genres[cite: 1]. 
* **Release Timing:** TV show additions peak strategically from June to September and in December[cite: 1].
* **Runtime Preferences:** The average movie duration falls perfectly between 80–120 minutes[cite: 1].

## 💡 Recommendations
1. **Regional Expansion:** Expand localized content in emerging markets (e.g., Japan, South Korea)[cite: 1].
2. **Release Planning:** Plan major releases in Q3-Q4 (June–Dec) to align with holiday viewership spikes[cite: 1].
3. **Format Diversification:** Diversify runtime formats by producing short documentaries and mini-series[cite: 1].
